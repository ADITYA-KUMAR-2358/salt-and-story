FICTIONAL DEMO DATA. The speaker is invented; the recipe is a classic.

Ragù is not a Tuesday sauce, ragù is a Sunday. Chop one onion, one carrot, one stick of celery very fine, that is your soffritto, and cook it in olive oil and a knob of butter until soft, a good twenty minutes, don't hurry it. Add five hundred grams of minced beef and two hundred of pork, brown it well, then a glass of white wine and let it vanish. A spoon of tomato paste, a tin of tomatoes, a little milk, and salt. Then the lid half on, the flame so small you can barely see it, three hours at least. Stir when you walk past. My mother said a sauce should blink, not boil. Serve with tagliatelle, never spaghetti, and a lot of parmesan.
