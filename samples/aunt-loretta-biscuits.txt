FICTIONAL DEMO DATA. The speaker is invented; the recipe is a classic.

Biscuits, honey, you got to have cold butter and a light hand. Two cups of flour, a tablespoon of baking powder, a teaspoon of salt, half a cup of cold butter grated in, and about three quarters of a cup of buttermilk. Stir only until it comes together. It should look shaggy and ugly. Pat it out an inch thick, fold it over twice, cut straight down, and don't twist the cutter, because twisting seals the edges and they won't rise. Put them close together in a hot pan, four hundred and fifty degrees, twelve to fifteen minutes. Brush with butter the second they come out. Mama made these before church every Sunday and the whole street knew.
