FICTIONAL DEMO DATA. The speaker is invented; the recipe is a classic.

Kartoffelsalat, the Swabian way, no mayonnaise, please. A kilo of waxy potatoes, boil them in their skins, peel them while warm, that is the secret, and slice them thin. One small onion chopped fine. Heat a cup of beef broth and pour it over the potatoes with three spoons of vinegar, a spoon of mustard, salt, pepper, and four spoons of oil. Mix gently. It should look wet, the potatoes drink it. Wait an hour before you eat. Chives on top. On Christmas Eve we had this with sausages and my brothers fought over the last bowl.
