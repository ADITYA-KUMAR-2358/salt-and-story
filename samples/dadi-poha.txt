Poha, easy, even you can do it. Take two cups of thick poha, wash it in a sieve, just water over it, don't soak, and let it sit and drain. Add salt and a spoon of sugar and mix with your hands, gently. In the pan, oil, then mustard seeds, then curry leaves, one onion chopped fine, and a handful of peanuts. Fry the peanuts till they pop. Turmeric, a pinch, and the poha goes in. Mix, cover for two minutes on low flame. Lemon at the end and fresh coriander. I make it every Sunday since your grandfather liked it, and he would say poha must be soft like a pillow, not dry like paper.
