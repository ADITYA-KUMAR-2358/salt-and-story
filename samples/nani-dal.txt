Okay beta, listen, the dal. Take one cup of toor dal, wash it till the water runs clear, three times at least. Put it in the cooker with a pinch of turmeric and water, about three cups, don't measure too much, you will know. Three whistles and then switch off and let it rest. My mother always said never open the cooker in a hurry, the dal should finish its own thinking. Now the tadka, this is the important part. Ghee in a small pan, a spoon, a little more if you are feeling generous. Cumin seeds, let them crackle, then garlic, four or five cloves, crushed, till it goes golden but not brown. Two dry red chillies and a pinch of hing. Pour it over the dal and cover it quickly so the smell stays in. Never skip the ghee at the end, it is the soul of the dal. Eat it with hot rice and a little lemon.
