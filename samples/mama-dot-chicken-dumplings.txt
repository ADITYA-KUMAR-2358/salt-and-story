FICTIONAL DEMO DATA. The speaker is invented; the recipe is a classic.

Chicken and dumplings is Sunday food. Put a whole chicken in the big pot with water to cover, an onion, two celery stalks, a carrot, salt and some pepper. Simmer it an hour and a half until it falls off the bone, then pull the chicken out, take the meat off and put it back in the broth. Dumplings: two cups of flour, a tablespoon of baking powder, a teaspoon of salt, a good spoon of butter rubbed in and about three quarters of a cup of buttermilk. Drop them by the spoonful into the simmering broth, put the lid on and do not lift it for fifteen minutes. Not once. If you peek they go heavy as stones. We ate this every time somebody was sick or sad or just home from the road.
