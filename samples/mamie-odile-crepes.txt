FICTIONAL DEMO DATA. The speaker is invented; the recipe is a classic.

The crêpes are for Chandeleur, the second of February, and you hold a coin in your left hand and flip with the right for luck. Batter: two hundred and fifty grams of flour, a pinch of salt, three eggs, half a litre of milk, a spoon of melted butter, and a splash of rum if no one is looking. Whisk the flour and eggs first, then the milk slowly so there are no lumps. Let it rest an hour, it must rest, like a person after lunch. Hot pan, a little butter, a ladle, tilt it, thin as lace, a minute each side. Sugar and lemon, that is all, anything else is a distraction. I still hear the pan sizzling when I think of my father at the stove in his undershirt.
