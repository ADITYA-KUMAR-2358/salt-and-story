# Code of Conduct

Salt & Story is about families, memory and food. We want the project around it to feel the same way: warm, patient and welcoming to everyone.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many contributors are new to open source.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect that people's families, cultures and recipes are personal. Never mock someone's food, language or family.
- Protect privacy. Never post anyone's personal details, voice recordings or family photos without their clear permission.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from it.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address, email or recordings, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, Aditya Kumar, privately through his GitHub profile: <https://github.com/ADITYA-KUMAR-2358>. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly. Please include links or screenshots where you can.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Responses are proportionate: a private note for a first misunderstanding, a ban for harassment.
